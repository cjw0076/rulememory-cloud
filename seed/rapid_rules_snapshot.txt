Google Cloud Rapid Agent Hackathon -- rules snapshot (demo source)

Submission deadline: 2026-06-11 14:00 PDT.
Internal freeze deadline: 2026-06-09 23:59 PDT.
Video upload deadline: 2026-06-11 13:00 PDT.

Build requirement: projects must use Gemini models on Agent Platform and Google
Cloud Agent Builder. All projects are required to integrate at least one
participating partner's MCP server.

Build requirement: the agent must move beyond chat and complete multi-step tasks
under user oversight.

Eligibility: participants must be above the legal age of majority in their place
of residence. Residents of certain sanctioned jurisdictions are not eligible.

Required submission assets: a hosted project URL, a public open-source repository
with a license visible at the repo top, and an approximately 3-minute demo video.
